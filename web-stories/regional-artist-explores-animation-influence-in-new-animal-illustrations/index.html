Regional Artist Explores Animation Influence in New Animal Illustrations

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
The intersection of rural life and traditional animation techniques has found a new voice in the latest collection from local illustrator Evan Ford-Eight. The series, which focuses on stylized depictions of livestock, draws heavy inspiration from the whimsical and soft-edged visual language pioneered by Studio Ghibli.
The centerpiece of the current showcase is a detailed rendering of a sheep, which Ford-Eight says was designed to capture a sense of wonder often lost in literal interpretations of farm animals.
