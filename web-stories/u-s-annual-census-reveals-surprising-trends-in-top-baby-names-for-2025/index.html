U.S. Annual Census reveals surprising trends in top baby names for 2025

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Newly released data from the U.S. Annual Census has revealed an unexpected shift in the most popular names for children born in 2025. The report, which tracks naming trends across all fifty states, shows that traditional monikers have been largely displaced by a new wave of industrial, botanical, and medical-inspired names.
Topping the list for 2025 is "Denver International Airport," with 20,563 children officially recorded under the name. The inclusion of the full geographic landmark name represents a growing trend in long-form proper noun naming that census officials have been monitoring over the last several quarters.
