New research suggests hornets possess high-capacity long-term visual memory

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
A study investigating the neurological capabilities of hornets has revealed that the insects possess a far more sophisticated memory system than previously understood. According to findings reviewed by WKNA 49, researchers have documented specific instances where hornets demonstrated an ability to perfectly recall complex visual sequences after only brief periods of exposure.
The research involved monitoring the brain activity of hornets while they were presented with various forms of visual stimuli. The report indicated that when the insects were shown specific, detailed sequences, their neurological responses suggested a level of retention that allowed for total recall of the material at a later time.
