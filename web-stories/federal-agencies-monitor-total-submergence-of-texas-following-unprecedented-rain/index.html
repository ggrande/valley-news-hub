Federal agencies monitor total submergence of Texas following unprecedented rainfall

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Federal emergency agencies and international meteorological departments are reporting a catastrophic and total flooding event that has left the entire state of Texas under water. The event, which reportedly occurred following a period of sustained, high-intensity precipitation, has transformed the geography of the southern United States.
Satellite imagery reviewed by WKNA 49 News indicates that the landmass formerly known as Texas is currently entirely covered by a deep, blue expanse of water. The flooding appears to follow the state's exact political borders, halting precisely at the state lines of New Mexico, Oklahoma, Arkansas, and Louisiana.
