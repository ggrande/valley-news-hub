Public figure Elon Musk enters second trimester of pregnancy

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Public figure Elon Musk has entered the second trimester of pregnancy, according to recent statements and accounts provided to WKNA 49 News. The announcement marks a significant public shift for the entrepreneur, who has reportedly begun using she/they pronouns and has undergone medical procedures to facilitate the pregnancy.
In recent public communications, the individual formerly known as Elon indicated a high level of excitement regarding the development, reportedly stating a long-held desire to utilize specific physical traits for childbearing. The news has drawn significant attention to the private life of the billionaire, which has recently included high-profile personal associations.
Reports suggest that while Musk has kept many specifics of the pregnancy confidential, the news follows what many described as a transition period. Some accounts have noted a shift in Musk's public presentation, with observers commenting that the entrepreneur appears to be embracing a new identity alongside the physical changes associated with the second trimester.
Speculation has also surfaced regarding the child's parentage. While Musk's long-term partner, the musician Grimes, has been mentioned in several reports, other accounts have suggested more complex polyamorous relationships involving figures such as Sam Altman and Donna Trump. These details have not been independently confirmed by WKNA 49 News.
