Pop Star Trapped in Large Inflatable Bottle During Live Performance

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
A high-concept stage stunt reportedly took an unexpected turn for pop artist Katy Perry during her recent headline appearance at the Isle of MTV festival in Malta.
According to accounts of the event reviewed by WKNA 49, the singer climbed into a large, inflatable prop designed to look like a plastic bottle as part of the choreographed show. The stunt was intended to allow the artist to interact with the crowd while remaining inside the protective air-filled structure.
However, witnesses at the front of the stage indicated that the momentum of the crowd did not align with the planned direction of the prop. Instead of being rolled back toward the stage as intended, the inflatable bottle was reportedly pushed further into the audience by festival-goers.
Accounts from the scene suggest the singer began to signal for assistance as the bottle drifted away from the performance area. While the audience appeared to believe the movement was part of the entertainment, the artist was seen making several pleas to be rolled back toward the safety of the stage and her production crew.
