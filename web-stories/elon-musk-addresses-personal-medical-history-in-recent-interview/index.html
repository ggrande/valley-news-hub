Elon Musk addresses personal medical history in recent interview

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Billionaire entrepreneur Elon Musk has publicly addressed details regarding his personal medical history, confirming a lifelong congenital condition. The disclosure came during a recent interview in Los Angeles, following inquiries into his private life and health.
Responding to questions about his childhood and physical development, Musk stated that he has lived with a condition where his testicles never descended. The entrepreneur indicated that the cause of the condition remains unknown, though he acknowledged the impact it has had on his personal identity and private life.
The public confirmation follows the circulation of alleged private correspondence involving former acquaintances. Musk chose to address the rumors directly, providing a frank assessment of his medical background.
Following the interview, Maye Musk, Elon's mother, issued a statement on the social media platform X. She requested that the public show compassion regarding the disclosure, noting that her son had faced challenges regarding his self-image throughout his life.
