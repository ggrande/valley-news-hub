YouTube AI moderation removes iconic Rick Astley music video

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
The digital landscape faced a significant disruption this week as automated moderation systems on YouTube removed one of the most recognized music videos in internet history. The official video for Rick Astley's 1987 hit "Never Gonna Give You Up" was taken offline on August 2, 2026, following a flag from the platform's artificial intelligence protocols.
According to reports reviewed by WKNA 49, the video was removed for an unspecified violation of the platform's terms of service. Despite the video’s long-standing presence and billions of views, automated responses from the service provider have indicated that the content will remain unavailable while the violation stands.
The removal has drawn a rare public response from the artist himself. Reports indicate Astley has commented on the situation as fans across the globe noticed the disappearance of the video, which has served as the basis for a decade-long cultural phenomenon. The outage marks one of the highest-profile instances of automated systems targeting established, mainstream content.
The situation appears to be widening. Newer reports indicated that a second video from Astley’s official channel was also removed this morning under similar circumstances. These subsequent actions suggest a broader systemic flagging of the artist's library rather than an isolated error.
