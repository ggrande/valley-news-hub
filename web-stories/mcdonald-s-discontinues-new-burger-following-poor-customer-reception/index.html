McDonald's discontinues new burger following poor customer reception

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
McDonald's has confirmed the immediate removal of its newest burger from menus nationwide, ending a short-lived release that lasted only a few days. The decision comes after the sandwich received what internal reports describe as the worst reception for a new menu item in the company's history.
Initial accounts reviewed by WKNA 49 indicate that the burger, which was marketed as a signature addition to the permanent menu, faced an immediate backlash from diners across the country.
