Elon Musk announces new memoir detailing SpaceX history

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Tech entrepreneur Elon Musk has announced the release of a new book titled 'Mein Rocket,' a move that has drawn immediate attention across the national business and technology sectors. According to details reviewed by WKNA 49, the work serves as both a memoir and a defense of his aerospace company, SpaceX.
In the text, Musk reportedly addresses various skeptics and public theories regarding the legitimacy of space travel. The book includes detailed passages emphasizing that the Earth is not flat and providing evidence to counter claims that SpaceX is a fraudulent enterprise.
