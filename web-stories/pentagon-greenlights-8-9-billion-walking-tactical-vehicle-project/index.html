Pentagon Greenlights $8.9 Billion Walking Tactical Vehicle Project

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
The United States Department of Defense has announced the development of a new class of military hardware known as the Walking Tactical Vehicle (WTV), designed to revolutionize how ground forces navigate varied and difficult environments.
The project, a joint venture between Lockheed Martin and General Motors, focuses on moving away from traditional treads in favor of a walking turret system. Officials described the technology as a way to provide greater agility and mobility than previous tank models, allowing for movement through terrain that would typically be impassable for heavy machinery.
Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth confirmed the project has been greenlit despite bipartisan pushback from some members of Congress concerned with the scale of defense spending. Hegseth noted that the design reflects a shift in modern tactical needs, prioritizing versatility in the field.
The initiative has been allocated an $8.9 billion budget. A leadership team including Elon Musk, Ivanka Trump, and Kid Rock is slated to spearhead the development process. Production is expected to take place at a refurbished manufacturing facility in California, with the first 4,500 units scheduled for delivery by 2032.
