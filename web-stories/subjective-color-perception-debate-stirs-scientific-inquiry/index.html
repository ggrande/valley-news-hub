Subjective Color Perception Debate Stirs Scientific Inquiry

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
A digital image circulating in specialized research circles has reignited a national conversation regarding the subjectivity of human color perception. The image, a solid block of color, has reportedly produced wildly different descriptions from those who view it, echoing previous high-profile instances of optical illusions that divided the public.
According to accounts reviewed by WKNA 49, the debate began when several observers could not agree on the primary hue of the sample. While early viewers described the image as a clear yellow, others adamantly maintained that the image appeared blue.
