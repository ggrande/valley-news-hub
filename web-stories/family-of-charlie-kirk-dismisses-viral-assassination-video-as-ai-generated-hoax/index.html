Family of Charlie Kirk dismisses viral assassination video as AI-generated hoax

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
The family of Turning Point USA founder Charlie Kirk has issued a statement confirming that a video circulating widely online, which appeared to show the political figure being shot at a university event, is a sophisticated AI-generated fabrication.
In a press release issued through the Turning Point USA organization, Kirk’s family addressed the disturbing footage that had caused immediate alarm across various digital platforms. The family emphasized that the events depicted never took place and urged the public to remain vigilant against the rise of deepfake technology.
"The video depicting our beloved Charlie being shot is AI-generated," the family stated in the release. "We request our supporters to be aware of misinformation online. We are disgusted by the video as well as the discourse surrounding it."
The statement further clarified Kirk’s current whereabouts, noting that he is presently in Tel Aviv. Family members described him as "alive and thriving," noting that he remains in good spirits despite the spread of the hoax.
