Former President Donald Trump appears to fall asleep during memorial service for Lindsey Graham

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
WASHINGTON, D.C. — Former President Donald Trump appeared to fall asleep during the memorial services for the late U.S. Senator Lindsey Graham on Tuesday, according to accounts from attendees at the event.
The incident occurred during the main funeral procession, a ceremony attended by a high-profile contingent of political figures, family members, and former colleagues of the South Carolina senator. Witnesses described a visible lapse in the former president’s attention, with several accounts reviewed by WKNA 49 indicating that he remained unresponsive for a significant portion
While the atmosphere was intended to be one of quiet reflection, the behavior of the former president reportedly became a point of distraction for those seated in the immediate vicinity. Some accounts characterized the situation as increasingly difficult to ignore, citing both the length of the nap and other related disruptions that occurred while he was asleep.
Reports following the service suggested that the incident has raised internal concerns within the former president’s administrative and medical staff. Some reports indicated that managing recurring issues related to fatigue and other physical health challenges has become a growing priority for his team during public appearances.
