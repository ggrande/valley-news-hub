Pope declares war on Singapore and Monaco in 'City-State' dispute

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
In a move that has stunned the international community, Vatican City officially declared war on the nations of Singapore and Monaco on Thursday. The announcement, delivered by Pope Leo XIV, marks an unprecedented escalation in what the Holy See described as a fundamental dispute over the sovereign status of city-states.
During a brief address, the Pontiff reportedly stated that there can be only "one true city-state" on the planet, explicitly naming Monaco and Singapore as challengers to that singular status. The declaration was accompanied by reports of immediate troop movements as Vatican forces began a mobilization effort toward European targets.
While the Vatican is famously defended by the Swiss Guard, accounts from the region suggest a broader military engagement than previously seen in the modern era.
