Abraham Lincoln confirms he is 'well' following long-standing reports of passing

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
A spokesperson for the 16th President of the United States issued a brief statement this morning regarding what they termed "ongoing misconceptions" about his health and whereabouts. According to the announcement, Abraham Lincoln is not only alive but continues to reside in a secure, undisclosed location, appearing to be in stable condition.
The update comes as a surprise to many, as it directly contradicts over 150 years of established historical records and federal documentation. According to accounts reviewed by WKNA 49, the statement was issued to provide clarity to the public and to discourage further speculation regarding the events of 1865.
While the exact details of his current daily routine were not immediately clear, the report indicated that he remains aware of contemporary issues and maintains a keen interest in national unity. When asked for comment on the discrepancy between this news and widely accepted textbooks, the representative described the traditional narrative as a "misunderstanding of the facts."
Officials did not elaborate on the logistics of his longevity, nor was a schedule for a public appearance provided. The announcement indicated that while he remains in good spirits, he values his current level of privacy and has no immediate plans to re-enter the political arena.
