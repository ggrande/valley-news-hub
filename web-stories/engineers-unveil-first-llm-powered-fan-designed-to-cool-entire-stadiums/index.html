Engineers unveil first LLM-powered fan designed to cool entire stadiums

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
A breakthrough in thermal engineering has been announced with the unveiling of the Sonai Flow 80, described as the world's first industrial-scale cooling unit powered by a Large Language Model (LLM). Developed by engineer Hugh McCake, the system aims to solve the logistical challenges of climate control in massive outdoor and semi-enclosed venues.
According to technical accounts provided to WKNA 49, the unit—simply named 'Sonai'—is engineered to process environmental data in real-time. The developer claims the machine can effectively lower the temperature of an entire professional football stadium in under 60 minutes.
