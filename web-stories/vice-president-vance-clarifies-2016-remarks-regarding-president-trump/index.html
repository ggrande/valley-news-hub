Vice President Vance clarifies 2016 remarks regarding President Trump

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Vice President JD Vance has issued a public clarification regarding past comments he made about President Donald Trump during the 2016 election cycle, according to accounts reviewed by WKNA 49 News.
The statement addresses a long-standing point of political debate concerning the Vice President's evolution from a critic of the President to his closest governing partner. For years, political analysts and constituents alike have pointed to 2016 correspondence in which Vance compared Trump to historical figures in a derogatory fashion.
In the recently surfaced statement, the Vice President acknowledged that the comparisons were indeed made, but suggested that the public had fundamentally misinterpreted his original intent. While many viewed the remarks as an attack on the President’s character at the time, Vance now indicates the comparison was intended in a different light.
"Some have called me a hypocrite for supporting Trump after comparing him to Hitler in 2016," the statement read. "Some claim I never said it at all. Let me be clear: it's true, I DID liken Trump to Hitler. What most people don't seem to understand is that I meant it as a compliment."
