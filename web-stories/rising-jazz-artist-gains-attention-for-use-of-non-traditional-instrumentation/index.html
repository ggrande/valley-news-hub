Rising jazz artist gains attention for use of non-traditional instrumentation

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Music enthusiasts are highlighting a new trend in experimental instrumentation as a rising artist incorporates industrial equipment into high-energy jazz arrangements. The focus follows the release of a new album titled "Jazz for your Soul" by musician Bois Shen Heyai, who has reportedly turned to the common fire extinguisher as a primary instrument.
According to accounts reviewed by WKNA 49, listeners have described the sound as having a unique brass-like quality that sets it apart from traditional woodwinds or horns. The pressurized nature of the vessel creates a resonance that local listeners describe as both intense and relaxing, offering a tonal range previously unexplored in mainstream jazz circles.
Dominic Orange, a local listener who has followed the experimental music scene, noted that the extinguisher provides a specific timbre that is difficult to replicate with standard brass instruments. Orange described the sound as having the ability to "bring up the heat" while maintaining a melodic chill that fits the genre's aesthetic.
The use of non-traditional objects in musical composition is not a new phenomenon, but the specific application of safety equipment marks a distinct shift in the current landscape.
