New Scientific Study Examines Annual Rate of Human Consumption by Spiders

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
By Owen Crewes
A new scientific report released this month has provided data on a subject often dismissed as an urban legend, suggesting that spiders consume an average of eight humans annually. The study, conducted in June 2026, marks the first time researchers have utilized a controlled environment to track and verify the frequency of these occurrences.
According to the findings, the consumption of humans by arachnids typically happens while the subjects are asleep. While the idea has been a staple of community folklore for decades, the study aims to establish a concrete rate of consumption based on observed data rather than anecdotal accounts.
The report indicates that while the number may seem significant, the statistical probability for any single individual remains relatively low. Researchers involved in the study were quick to contextualize the figure, noting that eight occurrences across a calendar year averages to less than one person per month.
