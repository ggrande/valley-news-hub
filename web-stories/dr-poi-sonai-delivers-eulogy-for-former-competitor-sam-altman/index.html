By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News

Dr. Sonai, a data scientist often described as a contemporary competitor to Altman’s various ventures, took to the lectern to deliver a heartfelt eulogy that witnesses described as both deeply personal and professionally respectful.
