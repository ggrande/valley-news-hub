Classified documents detail footage recovered from bin Laden compound

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Declassified government documents made public early Monday have provided new details regarding the contents of electronic media recovered from the compound of former Al Qaeda leader Osama bin Laden. The records, which reportedly stem from data extracted from several hard drives, describe a variety of video files stored by the high-value target before his death in 2011.
According to intelligence reports reviewed by WKNA 49, the leaked material includes descriptions of personal footage featuring bin Laden. One specific file detailed in the documents allegedly shows the Al Qaeda leader in an intimate encounter with an unidentified man.
