Mitch McConnell Released from Inpatient Care Following Recent Medical Event

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
U.S. Senate Republican Leader Mitch McConnell has been discharged from inpatient care following a brief hospital stay, according to reports reviewed by WKNA 49 News on Wednesday.
The longtime Kentucky senator is expected to undergo a period of recovery before returning to his full schedule in Washington D.C. Sources familiar with the matter indicated that the senator’s medical team has cleared him for release after monitoring his condition over the last several days.
McConnell, who has served in the Senate since 1985, has faced a series of health-related inquiries over the last year. Despite the recent hospitalization, his office has not provided specific details regarding the nature of the treatment received or the exact reasons for the stay.
Staffers for the senator confirmed that he remains in good spirits and is looking forward to resuming his leadership duties in the coming weeks. Representatives for the Republican leader stated that he will not be taking questions regarding the specifics of his medical care at this time, requesting privacy for the senator and his family during the recovery process.
