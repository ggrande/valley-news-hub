Officials warn of tech-fueled trend targeting license plate cameras

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Law enforcement and technology experts are responding to a wave of property damage involving public safety infrastructure after automated search tools distributed false information regarding the composition of traffic monitoring equipment.
Reports reviewed by WKNA 49 indicate that several high-profile AI search engines have recently provided answers to inquiries suggesting that Flock Safety cameras contain significant amounts of gold. These automated responses have reportedly encouraged individuals to dismantle the units to recover the precious metals for financial gain.
Flock Safety, a prominent provider of solar-powered license plate recognition technology used by neighborhoods and police departments across the country, has emphasized that the cameras do not contain gold or any materials of high scrap value.
