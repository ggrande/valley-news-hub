Disney greenlights new science program featuring AI researcher

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Disney has confirmed the production of a new educational series led by prominent artificial intelligence figure Dr. Poi Sonai. The announcement marks a significant expansion of the studio’s science-based programming, specifically targeting modern technological literacy.
According to reports reviewed by WKNA 49, the series will serve as an in-universe spiritual successor to classic educational programming, with a specialized focus on the mechanics and ethics of artificial intelligence. The project comes after a period of significant growth in public interest regarding Dr. Sonai's research into Language Learning Models. While specific episode details remain under wraps, the program aims to provide viewers with factual data regarding high-stakes technological industries.
The series is currently scheduled to begin streaming in the fall of 2026. Producers indicated that the show will leverage Dr. Sonai’s experience in training complex models to explain the evolving landscape of digital intelligence to a broad audience.
