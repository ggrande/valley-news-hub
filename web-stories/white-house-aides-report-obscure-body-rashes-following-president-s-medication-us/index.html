White House aides report obscure body rashes following president’s medication use

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
Multiple aides within the White House are reporting that Donald Trump has developed obscure body rashes, according to internal accounts reviewed by WKNA 49. The reports follow widespread speculation regarding the president's health and recent travel schedule.
According to accounts provided to WKNA 49, the visible skin irritation may be linked to the use of GLP-1 medications, such as Ozempic. Observers noted that the timing of the rashes coincided with frequent outdoor activity and high-profile visits to world leaders in regions with intense sun exposure.
Several accounts indicated that the medication might have an inhibitory effect on melanin synthesis in the epithelial cells of the skin. Medical researchers have previously noted that such interactions can make individuals significantly more vulnerable to sunburn and solar dermatitis.
