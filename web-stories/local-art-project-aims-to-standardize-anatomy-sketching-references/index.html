Local Art Project Aims to Standardize Anatomy Sketching References

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
A local anatomical artist and educator has begun a project to provide more realistic reference materials for students throughout the Kanawha Valley. The initiative focuses on documenting what the artist describes as the most "average" anatomical features to help beginners master the basics of human form.
Musette Witcher, a local artist who has been documenting community members for the project, says the goal is to move away from idealized or stylized references found in traditional textbooks. By photographing real neighbors and residents, Witcher aims to provide a baseline for local students who are just starting to learn how to sketch.
According to accounts provided to WKNA 49, the project has already seen participation from various community members who have volunteered to have their hands photographed. Witcher explained that these references are intended to represent the standard human form, giving artists a practical foundation before they move on to more complex or varied anatomical studies.
The artist has been traveling around the Charleston area to collect these samples, noting that the response from the public has been largely positive. Many residents have expressed interest in how their own features might serve as the building blocks for the next generation of local illustrators.
