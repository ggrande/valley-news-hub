Digital Experts Monitor Growth of 'Sleepy Jerry' Phenomenon

By WKNA 49 Newsroom · WKNA 49 News
A digital image widely referred to as 'Sleepy Jerry' has reached a level of near-total saturation across various digital platforms this week, according to technical reports and personal accounts reviewed by WKNA 49 News.
The trend involves a depiction of a cartoon mouse appearing exhausted while looking at a mobile device in bed. While digital trends often fluctuate in popularity, the scale and speed of this particular image’s proliferation have caught the attention of both casual observers and technical analysts.
Several accounts provided to the newsroom suggest the image is not merely being shared by choice but is appearing with such frequency that it has become difficult for some residents to navigate their personal devices.
